How transcript editing works

Correct captions changes the transcript and subtitles while keeping the original voice. Trim selection removes the selected words from audio and video using AI word timestamps. Replace speech regenerates the selected phrase using a synthetic voice, fitted to its existing duration. Shift-click to select a phrase, then apply changes to save and preview the result. Undo/redo and Revert to original keep edits recoverable. Advanced segment controls also let you adjust timings or cut a whole segment.

AI transcription sends the recording’s audio to OpenAI. AI speech sends the text of replacement segments to OpenAI and incurs API charges. It does not clone your voice or change lip movements. Exported MP4 includes selectable subtitles, and the in-app preview shows captions. Download your recording before closing the tab. Local media remains in the recordings folder until you remove it.
